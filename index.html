Why Sound Healing?

Sound healing is an ancient therapeutic practice that uses different frequencies and vibrations to improve physical, emotional, and mental well-being.

When these vibrations travel through the body, they help break up energetic blockages, promote cellular healing, and shift the brain from a state of active stress (Beta waves) to a state of deep relaxation and meditation (Alpha and Theta waves).

What to Expect During a Session

  1. 01

    Preparation

    You will lie down comfortably on a mat, supported by bolsters, pillows, and blankets to ensure total physical comfort.

  2. 02

    Guidance

    A short guided meditation or breathing exercise will help you settle into the space and set an intention.

  3. 03

    Sound Bath

    For 45–60 minutes, you will be immersed in a "bath" of sound as different instruments are played around you.

  4. 04

    Return

    The session concludes with a period of silence followed by a gentle invitation to return to the present moment.
